## Qualification score
Score every prospect 1–5 on:
- Operational pain
- Sensor/data fit
- Pilot accessibility
- Buying authority access
- Budget potential
- Reference value
- Expansion potential

Prioritize prospects with strong pain + pilot accessibility + reference value. Do not optimize solely for company size.

## First conversion goal
A qualified 20–30 minute discovery/demo meeting.

## Second conversion goal
A controlled paid or sponsored pilot with written acceptance criteria.

## Third conversion goal
A repeatable deployment package and reference customer.

## Messaging by vertical
Fire/rescue: “shared verified situational awareness when visibility and information are limited.”
Manufacturing: “one spatial evidence layer for people, equipment, facilities, and incidents.”
Construction: “capture and compare spatial conditions over time with provenance.”
Utilities: “federate field observations into a controlled spatial evidence record.”
Robotics: “give autonomous systems a sensor-fusion and evidence layer.”
Research: “open platform for reproducible multi-sensor spatial experiments.”
Security: “evidence-oriented spatial awareness without unsupported person-level inference.”

## Commercial discipline
Never claim that a prospect is a customer until a signed agreement exists. Never claim pilot results until measured. Never quote a guaranteed ROI before baseline measurements exist.
