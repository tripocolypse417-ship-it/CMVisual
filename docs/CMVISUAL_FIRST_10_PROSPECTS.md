## Pilot message

CMVisual is an early-stage spatial evidence platform that combines authorized sensor observations into a live, replayable world model. We are looking for one local organization willing to help evaluate a controlled prototype using real sensor data. The objective is to measure whether the system can improve situational awareness, spatial inspection, evidence preservation, or response workflow. We are not asking the organization to buy a finished product; the first conversation is technical discovery and pilot design.

## Rules for outreach

- Never claim the system currently sees through walls without a validated sensor providing such measurements.
- Never claim guaranteed lives saved or guaranteed accuracy.
- Never claim grant eligibility before confirming the company's legal structure and program rules.
- Ask for workflow problems first; demonstrate only the capabilities relevant to that workflow.
- Offer a controlled, measurable pilot rather than a broad sales pitch.
