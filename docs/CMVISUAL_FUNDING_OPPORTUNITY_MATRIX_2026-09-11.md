## Immediate priority — DoD FY26 Release 5

### 1. Navy SBIR — DON26BZ05-NV077
**Title:** Multi-Core Parallel Processing for Sensor Fusion Architecture
**Status:** Open
**Due:** September 23, 2026

Why it fits CMVisual:
- Explicitly targets multi-sensor fusion.
- Explicitly connects fusion to target tracking, situational awareness and decision-making.
- The stated technical problem includes latency, redundant processing and serial fusion architectures.
- CMVisual already has a platform-agnostic evidence/fusion architecture that can provide a commercial validation path.

Source: SBIR.gov topic 12860. The official solicitation must control final eligibility, technical requirements and submission instructions. citeturn4search1

### 2. DTRA — DTR26TZ05-NP001
**Title:** Novel Technologies for CWMD and Related Threats
**Status:** Open
**Due:** September 23, 2026

Why it is potentially relevant:
- The topic explicitly discusses using existing general-purpose military or commercial sensors such as cameras, microphones, motion detectors and passive infrared.
- It seeks novel detection approaches rather than requiring specialized purpose-built sensors.

This is a much narrower and higher-risk mission fit than CMVisual's civilian core. Do not claim CMVisual detects WMDs. Any pursuit would require a specific, scientifically defensible detection hypothesis and appropriate compliance/security review.

Source: SBIR.gov. citeturn4search0

**Action:** screen only; do not stretch the product claim to fit the topic.

## Missouri Technology Corporation

### 7. MTC IDEA Fund
The Missouri Technology Corporation's IDEA Fund is state-sponsored venture capital, not a grant. Current program structure includes TechLaunch up to $100,000, Seed Capital up to $500,000 and Venture Capital up to $2 million, with matching private investment requirements. The company must be headquartered in Missouri and have proprietary/protectable IP. citeturn1search0

The 2026 October-cycle deadline was August 4, 2026, so that cycle has passed. Continue preparing for the next available cycle rather than representing the 2026 October cycle as open. citeturn1search0

## Federal application readiness requirements

Before any SBIR/STTR submission:
- verify company legal status and small-business eligibility
- obtain/verify UEI and SAM registration as required
- confirm ownership/affiliation requirements
- confirm PI eligibility and required effort
- decide SBIR vs STTR
- identify an eligible research-institution partner if STTR
- protect/document IP ownership
- map the proposed work to one specific solicitation/topic
- prepare technical objectives, risks, milestones and measurable outcomes
- prepare commercialization evidence
- use the official solicitation as the controlling document

SBIR.gov states that a UEI from SAM.gov is required to receive SBIR/STTR awards and explains the SBIR/STTR distinction. citeturn3search2
