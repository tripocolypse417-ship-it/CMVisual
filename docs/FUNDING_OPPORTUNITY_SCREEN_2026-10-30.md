# WaveRadar Funding Opportunity Screen

**Research date:** 2026-10-30  
**Purpose:** Screen current public funding opportunities for strategic fit. This is not an application and does not assert applicant eligibility.

## 1. Executive summary

No funding application is submitted by this screen. The strongest route for WaveRadar is likely a partnership-led fire-service or university research proposal, because many fire grants are awarded to fire departments, public entities, universities, or eligible nonprofits rather than directly to an early-stage commercial vendor.

Two currently open federal solicitations were reviewed, but neither is an obvious direct fit for the current WaveRadar maturity:
- NIJ FY 2026 Drones as First Responders is open, but its stated purpose is rigorous evaluation of police drone-first-responder programs. It requires an operational law-enforcement partner and research/evaluation design. It is only relevant if WaveRadar has a genuine law-enforcement drone pilot to evaluate.
- EPA SBIR FY 2026 Phase I is open, but its topics are specific environmental technologies (including carbon-monoxide monitoring and PFAS sensing). It is relevant only if WaveRadar has a concrete, ownable sensor technology responsive to one of those topics. The registration process can take 6–8 weeks, making the November 8 deadline impractical unless registrations are already complete.

FEMA AFG and Fire Prevention & Safety FY 2025 application windows have closed. NIST SBIR currently lists no active NOFO. Monitor future cycles rather than misrepresenting closed opportunities as available.

## 4. WaveRadar funding strategy

### Near term
1. Establish the legal applicant entity and verify its SAM.gov, UEI, and small-business/nonprofit status before selecting an application.
2. Recruit an eligible fire department or university as a real research partner.
3. Complete the fresh Android baseline and one bounded, independently ground-truthed capability study.
4. Use the Validation Lab to produce a protocol, raw evidence manifest, limitations, and independent reviewer report.
5. Prepare a department-led future FP&S proposal focused on firefighter safety research, not a claim that WaveRadar is already operationally validated.

### Proposal package to prepare
- Specific problem statement and target user.
- One narrow, testable research question.
- Existing-product and literature review.
- Protocol, ground-truth method, and sample-size rationale.
- Human-subjects/IRB determination where applicable.
- Data-management, privacy, and cybersecurity plan.
- Hardware, calibration, and independent measurement costs.
- Evaluation metrics, uncertainty, and failure criteria.
- Partner roles, letters of support, and IP/data rights.
- Deployment, sustainment, and commercialization plan.

### Funding integrity rules
- Never apply to a topic merely because it is open; the proposed work must be responsive to the actual NOFO.
- Do not claim validated performance, deployments, partnerships, or matching funds that do not exist.
- Do not make a fire department or university a named partner until it has agreed in writing.
- Do not promise product procurement from a research award.
- Separate research costs, product-development costs, and commercialization costs in the budget.
- Verify every deadline, amendment, eligibility clause, indirect-cost rule, and submission portal immediately before applying.
