# CMVisual Commercialization Plan

## Positioning
CMVisual is an open spatial evidence platform for the physical world. It combines authorized sensor observations into a persistent world model while preserving source, time, location, uncertainty, confidence, validation state, and history.

**Pipeline:** Sensors → World Model → Evidence → Prediction → Human Decision

CMVisual does not claim unsupported through-obstruction sensing, identity, intent, dangerousness, psychology, or neural state. Nonvisual observations are shown only when supplied by validated external sensors and remain explicitly labeled.

## Priority sectors

1. **Emergency response / fire & rescue** — primary pilot sector. Focus on incident-site spatial awareness, sensor fusion, responder situational awareness, hazard evidence, replay, and controlled validation.
2. **Manufacturing / industrial operations** — recurring commercial market. Focus on inspection, facility awareness, robotics, equipment/worker spatial context, downtime reduction, and digital-twin workflows.
3. **Construction / infrastructure inspection** — mapping, progress verification, inspection, BIM/digital-twin integration, and hazardous-area awareness.
4. **Utilities / critical infrastructure** — persistent spatial monitoring, inspection, asset context, and field operations.
5. **Robotics / autonomous systems** — shared world models, multi-device coordinate alignment, uncertainty, trajectory history, and sensor integration.
6. **EMS / emergency management** — field coordination and evidence-rich situational awareness.
7. **Security technology integrators / law-enforcement technology** — only for evidence-oriented spatial tooling, with strict limits against unsupported person-level threat or psychological inference.
8. **Research / universities** — open experimentation, sensor-fusion research, model validation, and reproducible datasets.
9. **Defense** — later-stage opportunity after civilian validation and compliance maturity; do not make this the first commercial wedge.

## Commercial sequence

### Stage 1 — validation pilots
Sell controlled demonstrations and paid pilots before selling a finished platform.

Pilot structure:
- one site or incident scenario
- defined sensor inputs
- baseline workflow
- CMVisual workflow
- measurable outcome
- uncertainty/error report
- operator feedback
- repeatability test

Primary success metrics:
- time to establish spatial picture
- time to locate/inspect an observation
- sensor-fusion accuracy
- false-positive / false-association rate
- operator workload
- replay completeness
- data/provenance completeness
- deployment/setup time

### Stage 2 — recurring software
Open core remains useful and accessible. Paid enterprise features center on:
- multi-device federation
- private deployments
- enterprise administration
- persistent history/replay
- analytics
- APIs
- sensor integrations
- validation tooling
- compliance/audit controls
- support and deployment services

### Stage 3 — platform ecosystem
CMVisual becomes an integration layer for heterogeneous physical-world sensors and applications.

## Financial model

These are planning targets, not committed revenue or guaranteed outcomes.

| Stage | Commercial objective | Illustrative target |
|---|---|---:|
| Prototype → first pilots | 1–3 paid validation projects | $10k–$75k total |
| Early commercial | 5–10 customers | $100k–$500k ARR |
| Product-market fit | 20–50 customers | $500k–$2M ARR |
| Enterprise expansion | 50–150 customers | $2M–$10M ARR |
| Platform scale | Hundreds of deployments | $10M+ ARR |

Revenue should be separated from grants. Grants fund R&D; pilots prove willingness to pay; subscriptions/services create recurring commercial revenue.

## Funding route

NSF America's Seed Fund SBIR/STTR is a high-priority non-dilutive R&D route. The current NSF solicitation lists a November 4, 2026 full-proposal deadline and awards of up to $305,000 for Phase I and up to $1.25 million for Phase II, subject to program rules and funding availability. A Project Pitch invitation is required before a full proposal. The scientific-instrumentation pilot is also relevant to CMVisual's validated sensor-fusion/platform R&D direction.

## NSF Phase I technical thesis

Demonstrate that heterogeneous physical-world observations can be transformed into a persistent, uncertainty-aware spatial world model with:
- explicit sensor provenance
- shared-frame calibration
- data-quality quarantine
- cross-sensor conflict reporting
- measured/derived/predicted separation
- temporal replay
- prediction calibration
- human-readable evidence chains
- real-device validation

## Product moat

The durable asset is not a single detector model. It is the evidence and world-model layer:
- sensor adapters
- calibration
- provenance
- temporal history
- validation
- uncertainty
- federation
- replay
- prediction calibration
- interoperable APIs

## Non-negotiable product rules

- Real observations only.
- Never synthesize a person or sensor measurement.
- Never auto-align devices without an explicit shared coordinate frame and calibration.
- Never merge remote observations into local person identity without validated association.
- Keep measured, derived, corroborated, predicted, unresolved, and insufficient-data states distinct.
- Behavioral analysis stays at observable pattern level; it is not a dangerousness, intent, mental-health, personality, or criminality classifier.
- Physiological/neural claims require the appropriate validated sensor.
