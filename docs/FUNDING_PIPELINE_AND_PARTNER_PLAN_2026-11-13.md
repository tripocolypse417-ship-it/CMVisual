- Maintain one integrated funding pipeline across WaveRadar OS, the Research & Validation Lab, firefighter safety, sensor instrumentation, drone-assisted mapping, and commercialization.

- Do not represent WaveRadar as a registered LLC, SAM-registered entity, validated life-safety product, certified device, or proven sensor platform unless current documentary evidence supports the claim.
- Do not describe simulations, software tests, concept media, or historical observations as physical validation.
- Do not submit, sign, certify, accept terms, or commit match/cost share without the founder's review.
- Verify each forecast against the final NOFO when published. Forecasted dates and award ranges are not guarantees.

## Research concepts to reuse without scope drift

1. **Firefighter occupational-safety sensing feasibility (NIOSH R03):** controlled drills; time synchronization; source provenance; sensor completeness and dropout; environmental measurement quality; privacy and human-subjects protections; independent reference measurements; research outputs.
2. **First-responder occupational-health center contribution (NIOSH U19):** data infrastructure, reproducible sensor methods, exposure-context records, and transparent AI/digital measurement evaluation. Academic/occupational-health partners lead scientific hypotheses.
3. **Portable scientific validation instrument (NSF 26-511):** a repeatable measurement/acquisition instrument with defined sensor inputs, calibration/reference interfaces, timing, data integrity, and a credible commercial customer.
4. **WaveRadar OS commercial R&D (NSF 26-510):** interoperable, offline-capable, vendor-independent sensor/evidence backbone; measured/derived/inferred/predicted distinctions; uncertainty-aware outputs; independently testable interfaces.
5. **Law-enforcement DFR evaluation (NIJ):** separate, bounded police program evaluation with agency/research lead, cost-effectiveness, data governance, and operational metrics. Keep separate from firefighter product claims.

### Technical evidence and proposal discipline
- [ ] Freeze a narrow research question and measurable endpoints.
- [ ] Define independent ground truth, calibration, time synchronization, test conditions, failure modes, and sample-size rationale.
- [ ] Preserve raw evidence and append-only provenance.
- [ ] Keep sensor capability, registration, permission, sample arrival, freshness, and validated accuracy distinct.
- [ ] Do not include unsupported through-wall detection, victim localization, or life-safety accuracy claims.
- [ ] Separate software verification from physical validation.
- [ ] Create realistic, source-supported budgets; no invented vendor quotes, salaries, match, or institutional commitments.
