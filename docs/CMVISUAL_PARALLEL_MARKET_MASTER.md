# CMVisual — Parallel Market Master

## Core product
**CMVisual — The open spatial evidence platform for the physical world.**

Pipeline:
**Sensors → CMVisual → World Model → Evidence → Prediction → Human Decision**

The technical core stays the same across markets. Only the operational problem, proof metrics, buyer, deployment environment and commercial package change.

## Shared evidence contract
Every important observation/output carries:
**SOURCE · TIME · LOCATION · UNCERTAINTY · CONFIDENCE · VALIDATION · HISTORY**

Evidence classes:
- MEASURED
- CORROBORATED
- DERIVED
- HISTORICAL
- PREDICTED
- SPECULATIVE
- UNRESOLVED
- INSUFFICIENT DATA

No synthetic targets. No fabricated sensor observations. No unsupported through-wall claims. Person-level psychological, intent, hostility, dangerousness, identity or neural claims require appropriate validated evidence and are not inferred from ordinary appearance, gait, motion or radio signatures.

## Master pilot structure
### Phase 1 — Discovery
20–30 minute operational review with the prospective customer.

### Phase 2 — Controlled evaluation
Known environment, known sensor configuration, documented ground truth, explicit safety controls.

### Phase 3 — TEVV report
Measure accuracy, latency, coverage, false positives/negatives, sensor conflicts, prediction calibration, usability and failure modes.

### Phase 4 — Commercial decision
Choose one: stop, refine, paid pilot, deployment, research partnership or funding application.

# Market Track A — Fire / Rescue
**Buyer:** fire chief, training division, command staff, emergency-management partner.

**Operational problem:** fragmented spatial information during fire, rescue, hazmat and technical-rescue operations.

**Pilot proof:** spatial awareness, source visibility, uncertainty visibility, replay usefulness, latency, sensor coverage and crew usability.

**Initial offer:** controlled training/validation evaluation rather than a purchase request.

**Funding path:** department-led funding and applicable FEMA/USFA programs when current NOFOs permit; research/development framing may be relevant to qualifying programs.

# Market Track B — Search & Rescue / Emergency Management
**Buyer:** SAR organization, emergency-management agency, disaster-response team.

**Operational problem:** locating and coordinating observations across changing terrain and multiple devices.

**Pilot proof:** shared-frame alignment, coverage, track continuity, observation provenance, replay and physical forecast calibration.

**Commercial package:** field evaluation + multi-device deployment + support.

# Market Track C — Industrial / Manufacturing
**Buyer:** plant manager, EHS/safety leader, operations engineering, corporate risk.

**Operational problem:** fragmented visibility around workers, equipment, restricted areas and changing facility conditions.

**Pilot proof:** coverage, latency, event reconstruction, sensor conflict detection, workflow time saved and measurable safety/operational outcomes.

**Commercial package:** one-facility paid pilot → enterprise deployment → sensor/API integration and recurring support.

# Market Track D — Construction
**Buyer:** general contractor, site safety manager, project executive.

**Operational problem:** changing site geometry, worker/equipment awareness and disconnected site observations.

**Pilot proof:** site coverage, track continuity, replay, change detection, documented uncertainty and reporting usefulness.

**Commercial package:** project/site subscription or paid technical evaluation.

# Market Track E — Robotics / Autonomy
**Buyer:** robotics company, autonomy engineering team, integrator.

**Operational problem:** heterogeneous sensor streams must become a consistent spatial state for planning and evaluation.

**Pilot proof:** fusion latency, track continuity, coordinate alignment, sensor dropout recovery, forecast calibration and API stability.

**Commercial package:** SDK/API integration, evaluation tooling and enterprise support.

# Market Track F — Scientific Instrumentation / Research
**Buyer:** university lab, research institute, instrumentation company, government R&D organization.

**Operational problem:** researchers need time-synchronized, provenance-preserving multi-sensor data and repeatable validation.

**Pilot proof:** repeatability, measurement uncertainty, synchronization, calibration, replay and open data/export workflows.

**Funding path:** NSF SBIR/STTR 26-510 and the scientific-instrumentation pilot NSF 26-511 are active opportunities with a November 4, 2026 full-proposal deadline; Phase I requires the applicable Project Pitch invitation process.

# Market Track G — Critical Infrastructure / Utilities
**Buyer:** utility operations, infrastructure owner, resilience/security team.

**Operational problem:** geographically distributed observations and changing physical conditions must be combined without hiding uncertainty.

**Pilot proof:** shared-frame coverage, sensor provenance, anomaly/change detection, replay and operational response time.

**Commercial package:** controlled site pilot → multi-site deployment → private federation and audit tooling.

# Market Track H — Defense / Government R&D
**Buyer:** government R&D organization, prime contractor, defense technology integrator.

**Operational problem:** multi-sensor tracking and situational awareness under latency, compute and interoperability constraints.

**Pilot proof:** deterministic performance, latency, multi-sensor fusion, track continuity, hardware/edge constraints, cybersecurity and reproducible validation.

**Important:** pursue only opportunities with a genuine technical fit and the required registrations/security/compliance. Do not imply CMVisual is a defense capability merely because it can be adapted to one.

3. Search/rescue/emergency management — strong operational fit.
4. Construction — practical site pilot.
5. Robotics/autonomy — technical integration channel.
