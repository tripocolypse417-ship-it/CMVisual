# CMVisual Funding Reassessment — 2026-09-11

## Highest-priority opportunities

### 1. Navy DON26BZ05-NV077 — Multi-Core Parallel Processing for Sensor Fusion Architecture
- Open.
- Due September 23, 2026.
- Strong technical match to CMVisual's sensor-fusion architecture.
- Topic explicitly calls for modular, platform-agnostic multi-core sensor fusion, parallel spatial alignment, temporal correlation and attribute fusion, reduced latency, track integrity and scalability across multiple sensor sources.
- It also contains U.S.-ownership, foreign-influence, facility-clearance and personnel-clearance requirements for advanced/classified phases. Do not represent CMVisual as eligible for the classified phase until the company/security requirements are actually satisfied.
- Best use: treat this as a **screening candidate**, not a claimed submission.

### 2. NSF 26-511 — Scientific Instrumentation Pilot
- Active.
- Full proposal deadline November 4, 2026.
- CMVisual fits conceptually when framed as a next-generation spatial evidence instrument / experimental platform rather than a generic app.
- Phase I permits up to $305,000 for 6–18 months; Phase II up to $1.25M; Fast-Track up to $1.555M under the current solicitation.
- NSF states that the pilot targets next-generation instrumentation, novel experimental platforms and enabling technologies for AI-driven scientific advances.
- Critical eligibility issue: Phase I requires an official Project Pitch invitation. Do not claim full-proposal readiness until that invitation exists.

### 3. NSF 26-510 — Deep Technologies / SBIR-STTR
- Active.
- Full proposal deadline November 4, 2026.
- Phase I also requires an official Project Pitch invitation.
- The company must qualify as an eligible small business; NSF's current rules include a 500-employee size limit including affiliates. The PI must have primary employment with the small business at award and throughout the project, normally at least 51%.
- If pursuing STTR, a qualifying research-institution partner is required.
- Best route should be determined after company/PI/registration eligibility is confirmed.

### 4. Navy DON26BZ05-NV076 — Open, Layered, Yielding Modular Platform for Unified Systems
- Open; due September 23, 2026.
- Conceptually relevant because the topic emphasizes open modular architecture, data fusion, redundancy, interoperability, real-time analytics and commonality.
- Screen only; the topic is tied to Navy fleet architecture and government data-rights requirements.

### 5. Navy DON26BZ05-NV071 — Dynamically Generated, Articulated 3-Dimensional Training Content
- Open; due September 23, 2026.
- Relevant to the CMVisual Training Mode and real-world-data-to-3D training direction.
- This is a secondary opportunity, not the primary CMVisual funding thesis.

### 6. DARPA DPA26BZ05-DV019 — Semantically-Aware ISR
- Open; due September 23, 2026.
- Strong conceptual overlap with CMVisual's provenance, spatiotemporal reasoning, multimodal fusion, uncertainty and operator-audit architecture.
- The topic explicitly requires traceable evidence chains and excludes named-person identification, weapon-release authority and lethal engagement decisions.
- Do not imply CMVisual already meets the required embedded SWaP or contested-environment performance. Those are R&D objectives requiring measurement.

### 7. NAVWAR DON26BX05-NP004 — Unified Assured PNT Operational Awareness and Decision Support
- Open; due September 23, 2026.
- Relevant to the unified operational experience, common operating picture, confidence visualization, data integration, analytics and decision-support portions of CMVisual.
- It does not seek new PNT sensing technology; any pursuit would therefore be software/data-integration focused.

## Strategy change
Do not submit multiple speculative applications merely because topics sound similar. Select the opportunity where CMVisual can make a measurable, defensible technical claim with the least unsupported scope.

Current technical ranking:
1. NSF 26-511 — best civilian/scientific core fit.
2. NSF 26-510 — broad deep-tech commercial path.
3. DON26BZ05-NV077 — strongest sensor-fusion technical match, but significant security/ownership requirements.
4. DPA26BZ05-DV019 — strong future fit if embedded semantic reasoning and constrained-compute validation are developed.
5. DON26BX05-NP004 — strong unified decision-support fit.
6. DON26BZ05-NV071 — training-specific extension.
7. DON26BZ05-NV076 — broader systems-architecture extension.

## Registration / readiness gate
Before any federal submission, verify:
- legal company entity and ownership
- U.S. small-business eligibility
- PI employment and work authorization requirements
- SAM/UEI status
- SBA SBIR Company Registry status
- NSF registration status
- required Project Pitch invitation for NSF Phase I
- topic-specific agency registrations and security requirements
- actual measured technical readiness

No grant, investor, customer or award is considered secured until independently confirmed.
