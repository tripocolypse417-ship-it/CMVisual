# CMVisual — Grant / R&D Readiness

## Priority funding route

**NSF SBIR/STTR — NSF 26-510** is the primary near-term federal R&D route identified for CMVisual.

Current solicitation facts should be re-verified immediately before submission. The current solicitation lists a November 4, 2026 full-proposal deadline, Phase I funding up to $305,000 for 6–18 months, Phase II up to $1,250,000, and Fast-Track up to $1,555,000. NSF states that the program supports high-risk technologies with commercial impact. Eligibility and submission prerequisites must be confirmed before relying on these figures.

## Proposed technical thesis

**CMVisual will develop and validate an open spatial-evidence architecture that fuses heterogeneous real-world sensor observations into a common world model while preserving provenance, uncertainty, temporal history and validation state.**

The R&D problem is not simply object detection. The harder technical problem is determining how heterogeneous, imperfect observations can be aligned, reconciled and represented without converting uncertainty into false certainty.

## Phase I technical questions

1. Can heterogeneous observations be represented under a common evidence schema?
2. Can coordinate alignment be validated sufficiently for multi-device spatial projection?
3. Can measurement quality and uncertainty be propagated through the world model?
4. Can sensor disagreement be detected and exposed rather than hidden?
5. Can repeated observations support calibrated short-horizon physical forecasts?
6. Can the system preserve an auditable evidence timeline for replay?
7. Can emergency responders understand the resulting display under realistic workload?

## Proposed validation partner

A local fire/rescue organization is the preferred first operational validation environment because it provides a concrete safety-critical use case without requiring CMVisual to claim unsupported capabilities.

The proposed partner role is validation and operational feedback—not ownership of CMVisual intellectual property.

## Non-negotiable scientific claims policy

CMVisual must never present fabricated observations as measurements.

Do not claim:
- unsupported through-wall human detection
- identity from ordinary sensor signatures
- intent or dangerousness from gait/motion
- psychological or mental-state inference from ordinary sensors
- neural activity without a validated neural sensor
- precise geometry without an actual measurement source

## Commercial bridge

The first customer-development path is emergency response/fire & rescue. The same core architecture can later support industrial manufacturing, infrastructure inspection, robotics, utilities and research.

The commercial model should keep a free/open core while reserving enterprise revenue for multi-device federation, private deployments, history/replay, analytics, sensor integrations, validation tooling, administration and support.
